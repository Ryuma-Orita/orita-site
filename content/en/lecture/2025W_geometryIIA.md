**Textbooks**
- Y. Matsumoto, "多様体の基礎" (in Japanese), University of Tokyo Press

**Main references**
1. L. W. Tu, "An Introduction to Manifolds" (Japanese translation by M. Masuda, H. Abe, and T. Horiguchi, Shokabo)
2. Y. Matsushima, "多様体入門" (in Japanese), Shokabo

**Course contents**
1. Definition of manifolds
2. Examples of manifolds (on-demand lecture)
3. $C^{\infty}$ functions
4. $C^{\infty}$ maps
5. Tangent spaces
6. Differentials of $C^{\infty}$ maps
7. Examples of differentials of $C^{\infty}$ maps
8. Local properties of maps; exam
