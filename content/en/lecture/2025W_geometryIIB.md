- Y. Matsumoto, "多様体の基礎" (in Japanese), University of Tokyo Press

**Main references**
1. L. W. Tu, "An Introduction to Manifolds" (Japanese translation by M. Masuda, H. Abe, and T. Horiguchi, Shokabo)

5. Vector fields, part 2
6. Differential forms, part 1
7. Differential forms, part 2
8. Differential forms, part 3; exam
