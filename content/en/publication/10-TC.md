## Overview {#abstract}

How many continuous rules are needed to choose a path between any two points of a space? Farber's topological complexity measures this aspect of motion planning. This paper asks how the symplectic geometry of a manifold constrains that number.

We study closed, four-dimensional spherically monotone symplectic manifolds whose Kodaira dimension is not $-\infty$. For this class, we obtain estimates for topological complexity, connecting a question about motion planning with the geometry and topology of symplectic four-manifolds.
