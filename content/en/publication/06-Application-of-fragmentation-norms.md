## Overview {#abstract}

We study conditions on the values of a Hamiltonian function $H$ that force its time-one map $\varphi_H$ to move some point by a definite distance. The aim is to find a condition that remains valid under small uniform perturbations of $H$, without controlling its derivatives.

On a closed surface of positive genus, we give such a condition using two non-contractible simple closed curves. If $H$ is sufficiently large on one curve and negative on the other throughout the isotopy, some point moves by more than a fixed distance $\varepsilon>0$. The proof combines a lower bound for a fragmentation norm with its local boundedness in the $C^0$ topology. We also formulate a related problem about Hamiltonian pseudo-rotations.
